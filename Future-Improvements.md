# Future Improvements

The current circuit is an experimental prototype. Several improvements are planned for the next revision.

## Planned Changes

1. **Crystal Oscillator:** Replace the NE555 timing reference with a crystal oscillator for improved timing stability.

2. **Third Display Digit:** Expand the display from two digits to three digits.

3. **Improved Accuracy:** Investigate timing errors, counting synchronization, and signal conditioning.

4. **PCB Implementation:** Move from breadboard assembly to a custom PCB in a future revision.

## Development Goal

The ultimate goal is to develop a more accurate, stable, and compact digital frequency counter.
